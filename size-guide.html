Which size fits your bed?

Every duvet cover fits a bed of the standard 200 cm length, so choose by how wide your bed is.

  • Single 140 × 200 cm For beds 80 to 100 cm wide
  • Double 200 × 200 cm For beds 120 to 140 cm wide
  • King 240 × 220 cm For beds 160 to 200 cm wide

The 240 × 220 cm cover is also long enough for beds of 210 and 220 cm.
